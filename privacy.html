📄 1. Introduction

Welcome to V-Invoice ("we", "our", or "us"). V-Invoice is a professional invoice and quotation management application for Android and iOS devices.

This Privacy Policy describes how V-Invoice handles information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Our core design principle is local-first, offline-first privacy. Your business data belongs to you and only you.

🗃️ 2. Information We Collect

V-Invoice does not collect any personal information. The App operates entirely offline and does not communicate with any external server, cloud service, or remote database.

The following data is created and stored locally on your device only:

  • Company profile (name, email, phone, address, GST/Tax ID, logo, signature)
  • Client contact details (name, email, phone, address)
  • Product and service catalogue (descriptions, prices, tax rates)
  • Invoices and quotations (line items, amounts, dates, statuses)
  • Payment records and history
  • App preferences and settings (default currency, templates, etc.)
ℹ️
No account required. V-Invoice requires no sign-up, login, email address, or account of any kind. You can use the app immediately after installation without providing any personal information to us.

⚙️ 3. How We Use Information

All data entered into the App is used solely to provide the App's features to you, locally on your device:

  • Generating professional PDF invoices and quotations
  • Displaying your dashboard with revenue summaries and overdue alerts
  • Tracking payment statuses and partial payments
  • Generating financial reports and Excel (XLSX) exports
  • Sending payment reminders via your device's messaging apps (WhatsApp, SMS, Email)
  • Creating local JSON backup files for data recovery

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, analytics, machine learning, or any purpose beyond operating the App on your device.

🔑 4. Device Permissions

The App may request the following Android/iOS permissions. All permissions are used exclusively for local on-device functionality and are never used to transmit data externally.

Permission Why It's Needed Required

READ Media Images

🗄️ 5. Data Storage & Security

All App data is stored using React Native AsyncStorage, which maps to the device's internal sandboxed storage. This data:

  • Is accessible only to the V-Invoice application sandbox.
  • Is automatically removed if you uninstall the App (unless you export a backup beforehand).
  • Is not accessible by other apps on your device.
  • Is protected by your device's built-in hardware encryption (when device encryption is enabled).

We strongly recommend enabling device screen lock and encryption on your phone to ensure maximum protection of your financial data.

We have no access to your data at any time. If you lose your device without a backup, your data cannot be recovered by us.

🔗 6. Third-Party Services

V-Invoice uses open-source libraries to provide its features. None of these components collect user data or communicate with external analytics or advertising servers.

  • React Native — Core mobile framework (Meta / open source)
  • Local File System library — Local filesystem read/write only
  • Share / Intent library — Opens your device's native share sheet; no data interception
  • Document Picker library — Opens your device's file picker; no data transmitted
  • HTML-to-PDF library — Converts invoice HTML to PDF locally on device
  • WebView component — Renders invoice HTML locally; no browsing data collected
  • Google Fonts (CDN) — Fonts loaded for PDF rendering. Only a standard HTTP request for font files is made. No cookies or tracking. Can be disabled by blocking internet access.

We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase, Crashlytics, Facebook SDK, advertising SDKs, or any third-party analytics or tracking libraries.

🚫 7. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, lease, or share your data with any third party.

When you use the "Share as PDF" or "Send Reminder" features, you initiate the action through your device's native share sheet. The PDF or message is sent to the app of your choice (e.g., WhatsApp, Gmail). V-Invoice has no control over or access to the receiving application and does not log what was shared or to whom.

The only scenario where your data could leave your device is when you explicitly choose to share or export it (e.g., sending a PDF via WhatsApp, or saving a backup to cloud storage). These actions are initiated by you and under your full control.

💾 8. Backup & Export

V-Invoice provides an optional Export Backup feature that saves all your data as a JSON file to your device's Downloads/V-Invoice/ folder. This file:

  • Contains all your invoices, clients, products, and settings in plain JSON format.
  • Is created entirely on your device — no data is sent to our servers.
  • Can optionally be shared by you to cloud storage (Google Drive, etc.) — this action is your choice and responsibility.
  • Can be used to restore data via the Import Backup feature.

You are solely responsible for the security and storage of your backup files once they leave the App's local storage.

👶 9. Children's Privacy

V-Invoice is a professional business tool intended for users aged 13 and above. The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. Since we collect no personal data from any user, there is no risk of collecting children's data either.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used this App, please note that no data is transmitted to us and the data on the device is under your device's parental control settings.

⚖️ 10. Your Rights

Since V-Invoice stores all data locally on your device and we have no access to it, you have full and direct control over your data at all times:

  • Access: All your data is visible directly within the App at any time.
  • Correction: Edit any invoice, client, product, or setting from within the App.
  • Deletion: Use Settings → Clear All Data to permanently delete all App data from your device.
  • Portability: Use Settings → Backup Data to export all data as a JSON file.
  • Uninstallation: Uninstalling the App removes all data from your device permanently (unless backed up).

Users in the EU (GDPR), California (CCPA), and other jurisdictions with data rights legislation: because we collect no personal data and operate no servers, there is no data for us to provide, correct, or delete on your behalf. All control is local and in your hands.
